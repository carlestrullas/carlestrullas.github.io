Selected work

Anomaly detection for purchase orders

NTT Data, 2026

Flags abnormal ordering patterns early, before they have an operational impact, so the team can intervene on the cases that matter most across the order-to-cash process.

  • Supervised demand-forecasting models estimate the expected order at customer–SKU–day level; statistically significant deviations become candidate anomalies.
  • End-to-end pipeline joining historical orders, contextual features and feature engineering, built to scale across the full catalogue.
  • MLOps in place from day one: scheduled retraining, champion–challenger evaluation and CI/CD.
  • Python
  • PySpark
  • Databricks
  • Forecasting
  • MLOps

Multimodal search engine for points of sale

UVE Solutions, 2024–2026

Finds the points of sale whose photos and reviews are most similar to an image or a short text query. Deployed in production and integrated into the company's main platform.

  • CLIP-family models (SigLIP, Jina) embed images and review text into a shared space, with GPUs to speed up embedding computation.
  • PostgreSQL with HNSW indexing for fast approximate nearest-neighbour search.
  • Production API on Kubernetes, plus an internal Streamlit front end used by the data science team and in client demos.
  • Python
  • Hugging Face
  • PostgreSQL
  • Kubernetes
  • Streamlit

Analytics for consumer packaged goods clients

UVE Solutions, 2024–2026

Tailored models for international CPG companies to find the points of sale with the most potential, using regression, Naive Bayes, tree ensembles, XGBoost, neural networks and clustering. Data came from scrapers I built and maintained with BeautifulSoup and Selenium, processed through ETL pipelines on Azure Data Factory, Databricks and Cosmos DB.
